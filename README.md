# Business Analytics Portfolio

## Overview

This repository contains three independent projects completed as part of postgraduate studies in Business Analytics. Each project demonstrates different stages and applications of data analytics, including data preparation, exploratory data analysis, data visualisation, business interpretation, and business intelligence reporting.

The projects use separate datasets and address different analytical topics. Python is used for data preparation and visual analysis, while Microsoft Power BI is used for interactive business intelligence reporting.

## Projects

### Part 1 – Data Cleaning and Preparation

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Jupyter Notebook

This project focuses on preparing a deliberately messy retail sales dataset for further analysis.

The dataset contains fictional retail matcha transactions and was synthetically generated for educational purposes. It includes intentionally introduced data quality issues.

**Key activities:**
- Dataset exploration and structural assessment.
- Identification and treatment of missing values.
- Duplicate record detection and removal.
- Standardisation of inconsistent categorical values.
- Correction of incorrectly formatted numerical values.
- Data type conversion and validation.
- Export of the cleaned dataset.

### Part 2 – Data Visualisation and Insights

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

This project explores retail sales performance using a dataset containing 384 transactions across four geographical regions and four product categories during 2025.

Five visualisations were developed:

1. Monthly Revenue Over Time.
2. Revenue by Region.
3. Revenue by Product Category.
4. Distribution of Revenue.
5. Revenue Distribution by Product Category.

**Key Findings:**
- Monthly revenue increased by approximately 33% between January and December 2025.
- The West region generated the highest total revenue, while the North recorded the lowest.
- Electronics was the strongest revenue-generating product category.
- Clothing had the lowest median revenue per order.
- Revenue distribution was positively skewed, indicating the presence of higher-value transactions.

**Business Recommendations:**
1. Improve Clothing category performance through targeted promotions, product bundling, and cross-selling.
2. Investigate regional performance differences and use revenue trends to support inventory and staffing decisions.

The analysis covers one year only, so the observed revenue increase should not be interpreted as evidence of recurring seasonal growth. Revenue also does not represent profit.

### Part 3 – Power BI Business Analytics Case Study

**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI

This is an independent business analytics case study focused on the global data-related job market.

The report analyses 478,895 job postings and explores:

- Job posting trends over time.
- Demand across different data-related roles.
- Annual and hourly salary distributions.
- Educational degree requirements.
- Employment and remote-work characteristics.
- Geographical distribution of job opportunities.
- Quarterly changes in job demand and salaries.

The deliverable is an interactive Power BI report (`.pbix`), supported by screenshots of the report pages.

## Limitations

- The matcha retail dataset in Part 1 is synthetically generated and does not represent actual business transactions.
- The visual analysis in Part 2 covers transactions from 2025 only.
- Revenue-based findings do not account for product margins, operating costs, or profitability.
- The Power BI case study analyses job postings and reported salary information; salary currency units and data coverage should be considered when interpreting the results.
- Business recommendations should be validated against additional operational and financial data.

## Purpose

These projects demonstrate practical skills developed through postgraduate studies in Business Analytics, including data preparation, exploratory analysis, statistical and visual analysis, business interpretation, and business intelligence reporting.
